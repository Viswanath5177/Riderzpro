## Review outcomes

For each state/vehicle combination, document the permitted outcomes (`approved`, `needs_correction`, `rejected`), applicant-facing reason codes, evidence reviewers must record, escalation path, appeal/contact path, and target review time. Avoid storing identity-document images or unnecessary identifiers in reviewer notes.

## Privacy and security sign-off

- List each collected field/document, purpose, access roles, storage location, retention trigger, deletion method, and audit event.
- Confirm the exact privacy notice and consent language for each collection purpose.
- Confirm document access, malware scanning, breach response, applicant correction/deletion requests, and backup deletion behavior.
- Record the legal/operations owner and approval date for each state matrix version.

## Legal review references

Use current official sources and obtain counsel review for the actual operating model. The Motor Vehicles Act's licence, registration, and insurance provisions are relevant to motor vehicles; they should not be mechanically applied to pedal bicycles. State/vehicle applicability and any other employment, aggregator, local, or transport requirements remain to be assessed for the launch design.
