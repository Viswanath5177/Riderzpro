Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about becoming an EV seller? We're here to help.

What documents are required to start selling on Riderzpro? ▼
You need a valid GSTIN, business PAN card, an active bank account for payouts, and basic store contact details. Sole proprietorships, partnerships, and private limited companies are all eligible.
How does product verification and approval work? ▼
When you create a new product listing in your Seller Dashboard, it enters the Support Admin queue with 'pending_approval' status. Our certified engineers verify vehicle fitment, voltage, and battery safety guidelines before making it live to customers.
How does order pickup and delivery work? ▼
When a customer places an order, you receive an instant alert to accept and pack the item. Once you mark it 'Ready for Pickup', a certified Riderzpro delivery rider is automatically dispatched to your shop with live GPS tracking.
When will I receive payment for my completed sales? ▼
Payments are deposited directly into your verified bank account within 7 working days from order delivery, with detailed statements in your Seller Dashboard Payouts tab.
